## Destination

**Purpose.** The question or capability this serves, and why now.

**Observable outcome / acceptance criteria.** Numbered, each one checkable, and each naming the evidence that settles
it. This is what "done" means — not a checklist of steps completed.

**Non-goals.** What this effort deliberately excludes. An unstated exclusion is later read as an oversight.

**Authorization boundaries.** Who granted what, when, and what the grant does not cover. Selecting a plan is not
permission for every operation inside it, and nothing is granted that was not said.

**Baseline.** The revision, dataset identity or device state this starts from — measured, not remembered. Re-verify it
before continuing, and reconcile a mismatch instead of repairing it silently.
