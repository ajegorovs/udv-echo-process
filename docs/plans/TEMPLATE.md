# <Task name> — task plan

> Copy this file to `docs/plans/<slug>.md` and fill it in. One plan per bounded task; delete nothing, correct with a
> dated line. Register the plan in [`PLANS.md`](../../PLANS.md) when it is selected.

**State:** draft | ready for review | authorized | in progress | blocked | complete | superseded | cancelled

**Purpose** — what question or capability this task serves, in one paragraph. Say why now.

**Scope / exclusions** — what is in, and what is deliberately not. Exclusions are part of the plan; an unstated
exclusion is later read as an oversight.

**Authorization** — who granted execution, when, and exactly what was granted. Selection in `PLANS.md` is not
permission. Where nothing was granted, say so and stop at the decision.

**Baseline** — the revision, dataset identity or device state this plan starts from, measured rather than remembered;
recheck it before executing, and reconcile a mismatch instead of repairing it silently.

**Context a fresh reader needs** — the facts that would otherwise have to be reconstructed from a conversation:
documents to read first, existing behaviour, prior findings, and the traps that cost time last attempt.

## Steps

Numbered, concrete, each with the artefact it produces. Keep them small enough that one can be finished and verified
without the next one being decided.

## Expected results

What each step should produce, stated so a result can disagree with it.

## Validation

The commands, checks or instrument readings that decide whether this task passed — including the negative controls
where a check could pass for the wrong reason. Name what cannot be verified from here.

## Recovery

What to do when a step fails or is interrupted: what to restore, what to leave alone, and what stops the task for an
outside decision.

## Progress

Dated entries in place: what actually happened, what was measured, what is left. Never rewrite an earlier entry.

## Surprises & discoveries

Anything that contradicted the plan — including a finding that makes a later step wrong.

## Decision log

Each decision with its date and its reason, so a rejected option is not silently re-proposed.

## Outcomes & retrospective

At closeout: what was achieved, the evidence, the limits, what was left undone, and where durable findings were
carried to. Then clear the selection in `PLANS.md`.
