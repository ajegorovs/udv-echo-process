# SA5 — sitting-level setting effects and agreement (prespecification)

> **Status:** design only. No SA5 effect values, verdicts, floors or parameter rankings are computed here. This contract is frozen before inspecting SA5 contrasts. The two mixer-enabled sparse sittings are the scientific realizations; the zero-signal pass is acquisition evidence and Stage-2 E20/E64 is contextual paired evidence, not a third interchangeable sitting.

## Question and observation unit

For each sitting separately, which changes in burst, pitch and emissions alter the signed line-of-sight velocity profile, its fluctuations or recurrence, and its low-frequency spectral allocation? Does the *same oriented effect* recur in the other sitting? The matrix is **setting contrast × observable × physical depth × sitting**. A recording is a realization of its achieved condition; profiles and gates are correlated observations, not independent replicates. Report the two sitting-specific tables before computing any agreement summary. Do not infer an optimal setting, a population effect or the cause of a CFD discrepancy.

## Frozen inputs and comparability

Bind each file to pass, planned job, point, acquisition order, source digest and decoded achieved settings using the shared sparse ingest and the committed per-pass inventories. Reuse WP1–WP4 definitions and evidence without rewriting their published artefacts. Assert the relevant achieved settings and common view before each contrast; refuse a pairing with unintended independent changes instead of treating a requested label as proof. Retain each job's beginning/middle/end block-local anchor controls and the reference-condition runs in acquisition order. Those controls characterize *their own* job condition, not a pooled reference. The first sparse pass does not contribute signal effects. Stage-2 E20/E64 may be quoted with its own pair orientation and contemporaneous floor, but never pooled into a sparse-sitting effect or floor.

Primary profile and fluctuation comparisons use the declared leading **0–12 s** `primary-comparison` view, refusing short coverage. Recurrence and spectral allocation also use the separately labelled `full-record` view, with its differing duration, achieved sampling interval, resolution and Nyquist displayed; do not exchange these views in one contrast or confuse a full-record difference with a fixed-duration one. Exploratory cuts never enter the prespecified tables. Use the intersection of participating physical depth extents; between-grid knots are no finer than the coarsest participating pitch. Preserve native gate values and validity masks; publish the support bounds, knots, interpolation rule and participating source gates. No extrapolation, invented fine structure or comparison of different gate indices as if they were the same depth. Derivatives, if later warranted, are estimated on native grids first.

## Named observables

Keep an effect profile for every applicable metric before any depth reduction:

1. **Signed profile:** per-depth time mean in mm/s, plus the native mean profile's shape as a profile-level comparison. Keep signed differences; do not turn magnitude into an unsigned velocity without naming that new endpoint. Existing WP mean-profile contrasts and floors remain their own endpoint and context, not an automatic gate for other observables.
2. **Fluctuation scale:** per-depth standard deviation and median absolute deviation as separately named metrics in mm/s, using SA1's established definition/scaling and the same view on both sides. The median and IQR may be retained as descriptive profile-shape companions, not silently substituted for fluctuation scale.
3. **Recurrence:** SA1's mean-centered normalized ACF and its supported 1/e decay and descriptive recurrence-peak lag, each with its estimator settings, lag resolution and refusal state. Compare a scalar at depth only if *both* conditions define that scalar on a common supported lag domain; undefined constant/degenerate traces do not become zero. ACF-curve comparisons, if used, must state a common lag grid and endpoint separately from peak-lag differences. Do not declare an approximately 1 s physical period from a descriptive peak.
4. **Spectral allocation:** SA2.4 `band_fraction` Φ for the fixed closed 0–1 Hz band, separately by view and supported depth, with its mean detrend, admitted uniform-grid periodogram, periodic Hann taper and fractional cell-overlap convention unchanged. Preserve `defined`, `defined-zero-power` and `refused-axis` as distinct states. Only two defined fractions form a difference; a missing fraction is not zero. Report each side's rate, Nyquist, resolution and support alongside it. The SA2.4 repeat ranges are descriptive, **not** a screening floor.

Do not add total spectral power as a primary SA5 endpoint merely because SA2.4 publishes it. It has power units and a distinct interpretation from Φ; adding its contrast requires a separately reviewed amendment specifying its view, normalization, same-condition treatment and reason it is not redundant with fluctuation scale. No frequency-peak search or post-hoc gate selection enters the primary matrix.

## Oriented within-sitting contrasts

The following **operand membership and reduction are fixed identically for live-1 and live-2**. Bind by planned job, point label and decoded achieved settings; the same labels and acquisition orders occur in both sittings (`reports/sparse-mixer-live-{1,2}/points.csv`). The fine pitch requested as 0.617 mm is stored as 0.616666666667 mm. Each `M_record(z)` is the named observable computed once on that recording's declared view at its supported native gates; no pooling of time samples across recordings.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pitch at burst 4 | burst-4 / cc1 / 2 | burst-4 / cc3 / 4 | One scientific recording on each side |
| Pitch at burst 18 | burst-18 / cc2 / 8 | burst-18 / cc4 / 10 | One scientific recording on each side |

The pitch × burst interaction is `[M_cc4 − M_cc2] − [M_cc3 − M_cc1]` at each common knot, not a fifth selection. Corner knots are the common cc3/cc4 coarse native depths (2.96 mm spacing); follow the frozen WP3 nearest-native-gate alignment without interpolation, and report the physical gate offset and common support. The reference-pitch (1.85 mm) burst-job controls are context, never corner operands. All emissions operands have burst 10, 1.85 mm pitch, 50 native gates and the same declared window; align on their shared native depths inside the common support, without extrapolation.

**E20 reduction is a new SA5 effect endpoint, not a restatement of WP4's individual E20-run comparisons.** At each depth and for each metric/view separately, compute `M_E20(z) = [M_cr1(z) + M_cr2(z) + M_cr3(z) + M_cr4(z)] / 4` with equal recording weights, *after* per-record estimation and common-depth alignment. Every one of cr1–cr4 must define that metric at the knot: no available-case mean, substitution, nearest-in-order choice or interpolation in acquisition time. If any member is undefined there, the E20 operand and corresponding contrast are undefined at that knot with the member and reason shown. The e8/e64/e128 operand is **only its scientific recording**; no `ctrl-begin`, `ctrl-mid` or `ctrl-end` is averaged into any effect operand, even though those three controls have the same achieved condition within their job. Those controls separately describe block-local variation and bracket the scientific row; cr1–cr4 are four distinct common-reference jobs and their individual results remain visible as repeat/sequence context. Thus the effect operand and repeat-variation description are different decisions. Do not count an emissions job's four recordings as four independent runs or turn their range into a floor.

Preserve each sitting's acquisition sequence, bracket anchors and reference changes next to every contrast. Do not interpolate drift in clock time when per-recording clocks are unavailable. If a contrast cannot be bound to the same achieved condition apart from its named factor, mark it unavailable with the mismatch, not as a measured zero.

For any scalar profile `M(z)`, orient the effect as `M(high,z) − M(low,z)` as above. This orientation applies even when the high condition was acquired first. For recurrence time endpoints the subtraction has units of seconds; for Φ it is a dimensionless difference, not a relative percentage gain. Do not subtract ACF curves or PSD arrays under the name of a scalar contrast.

## Depth summaries, repeat variation and agreement

Publish `E(z)` with its valid common knots and units first. Then give the signed depth average, RMS effect magnitude, sign fractions over defined nonzero knots, and the depth and signed value of the largest absolute effect (ties resolved by shallower depth); state the knot weighting or integration rule and valid count. For profile shape, report correlation only when both compared profiles are nonconstant and sufficiently supported; otherwise `undefined` with reason. Signed averaging can cancel spatially changing effects, so it is never the sole magnitude summary. Do not call a maximum a robust effect or interpret a peak position when the profile is flat/undefined.

Describe same-whole-condition repeat differences separately for each **sitting × observable × view × condition**, with members and acquisition order named. Decide any resolvability threshold by endpoint, before applying it: it must compare like-for-like reductions, include both contrast sides' relevant variation and say which endpoint (depth-local, maximum or depth-averaged) it bounds. Existing velocity mean-profile floors apply only to their published endpoint and sitting. SA2.4 Φ repeat min/max/range is descriptive only; it is not promoted to a floor. If replication, support or a defensible metric-specific rule is lacking, label the effect **descriptive / not resolvable with this design** and identify what is missing. Never treat absence of a resolved difference as equivalence, or count gates/profiles as replicates. No p-values over gates or profiles and no post-hoc floor chosen to fit the observed effect.

After both sitting tables, compare the same oriented contrast and endpoint on their intersecting physical support: per-depth `E_live2(z) − E_live1(z)`, its RMS and signed depth-average difference, sign agreement on defined nonzero knots, profile correlation when nonconstant, and whether the largest-absolute-effect locations recur at the common grid's resolution. Preserve each sitting's own magnitude and anchor context next to these summaries; an agreement number alone cannot say whether both effects are large, both negligible or both unresolved. Use `recurs descriptively`, `does not recur descriptively`, or `not comparable/not resolvable` with the explicit support or variability reason; these are two realizations, not population inference.

## Publication and refusal gate

The implementation PR must test the exact seven table bindings in **both** sittings, E20 equal-weight four-run reduction versus any single/reference/nearest/bracket choice, exclusion of all block anchors from effect operands, missing/undefined E20 member refusal, membership/orientation (including reversed acquisition order), achieved-setting mismatches, common support and coarsest pitch, both view labels, spectral/recurrence refusal and zero-power cases, constant-profile correlation, sign-changing effects and endpoint-specific repeat handling. Generate deterministic reviewer-readable per-sitting tables/profiles first, then a separate cross-sitting agreement artefact, all with source digests, exact units, rules, generator revision and regeneration commands. Byte-reproduction tests must write to scratch outputs without modifying frozen WP or SA2.4 reports. Any changed endpoint or extra observable is an explicit amendment to this prespecification before its effect values are examined. SA3/POD, SA6 UI and CFD alignment remain separate evidence-driven slices.
